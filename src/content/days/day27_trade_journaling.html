📚 Why Keep a Trading Journal?

1. Track Your Progress

A trading journal helps you:

  • See your improvement over time with hard data
  • Identify which strategies work best for you
  • Understand your win rate and profit factor
  • Monitor your consistency across different market conditions

2. Identify Patterns & Mistakes

You'll discover patterns like:

  • ✅ You trade better in the morning vs. afternoon
  • ✅ Certain setups have higher win rates
  • ❌ You overtrade after a losing streak (revenge trading)
  • ❌ You exit winners too early but hold losers too long

3. Emotional Awareness

Your journal tracks not just what you traded, but how you felt:

  • Were you confident or uncertain?
  • Did you follow your plan or deviate?
  • What triggered emotional decisions?

4. Accountability & Discipline

Writing down every trade creates accountability. You can't lie to yourself when the data is right there!

📈 Key Performance Metrics to Track

1. Win Rate

Win Rate = (Number of Winning Trades ÷ Total Trades) × 100% Example: 35 wins out of 50 trades = 70% win rate

2. Profit Factor

Profit Factor = Total Winning $ ÷ Total Losing $ Example: $10,000 in wins ÷ $4,000 in losses = 2.5 profit factor (Above 1.5 is good, above 2.0 is excellent)

3. Average Win vs. Average Loss

Ideal Ratio: Average Win should be at least 1.5x your Average Loss

If your average win is $300 and average loss is $150, you have a 2:1 ratio ✅

4. Maximum Drawdown

The largest peak-to-trough decline in your account. Keep this under 20% for healthy risk management.

5. Expectancy

Expectancy = (Win Rate × Avg Win) - (Loss Rate × Avg Loss) This tells you your average expected return per trade

💡 Extra Learning Notes

Advanced Journaling Techniques

1. Weekly Review Process

Every Sunday, review your week:

  • What were my best trades and why?
  • What were my worst trades and why?
  • Did I follow my trading plan consistently?
  • What pattern am I noticing?
  • What will I improve next week?

2. Monthly Deep Dive

Once a month, analyze:

  • Strategy Performance: Which strategies performed best?
  • Time Analysis: What time of day are you most profitable?
  • Market Conditions: Do you perform better in trending or ranging markets?
  • Risk Management: Are you sticking to your position sizing rules?

The "Rule of Three"

After every trade, immediately write down:

  1. What went right (even in losses)
  2. What went wrong (even in wins)
  3. What you'll do differently next time

Screenshot Everything

Take screenshots at these key moments:

  • Before entry (what you see)
  • At entry (your execution)
  • During the trade (key moments)
  • At exit (final result)

Months later, these screenshots help you remember your exact reasoning!
