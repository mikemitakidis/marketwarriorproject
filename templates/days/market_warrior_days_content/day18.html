🔍 Part 1: Understanding Backtesting

What is Backtesting?

Backtesting is the process of applying trading rules to historical data to determine how a strategy would have performed. It's like a time machine - testing your system on the past to predict future performance.

🎯 Goals of Backtesting:

  • Validate strategy: Does it actually work?
  • Calculate win rate: What % of trades are winners?
  • Measure profitability: How much money does it make?
  • Identify weaknesses: When does the strategy fail?
  • Optimize parameters: Best MA periods, stop loss %, etc.
  • Build confidence: Trade with conviction knowing it's proven
Aspect Without Backtesting With Backtesting Confidence ❌ Hope & guess ✅ Data-driven conviction Win Rate ❌ Unknown ✅ 60% (proven) Drawdown ❌ Surprise -30% loss ✅ Expected -15% max Psychology ❌ Panic on losses ✅ Stay calm (normal drawdown) Optimization ❌ Random changes ✅ Data-based improvements

⚠️ The Backtesting Paradox

Past performance does not guarantee future results! However, a strategy that worked 200 times in the past is statistically more likely to work than one you just made up. Backtesting doesn't predict the future, but it dramatically increases your odds.

💡 Real Example: MA Crossover Backtest

Strategy: 20/50 SMA crossover on daily charts

Test Period: S&P 500 from 2020-2024 (4 years)


Results:

  • Total Trades: 47
  • Winning Trades: 28 (60% win rate)
  • Losing Trades: 19 (40% loss rate)
  • Average Win: +4.8%
  • Average Loss: -2.1%
  • Net Profit: +87% over 4 years
  • Max Drawdown: -18% (5 losses in a row)

Conclusion: Strategy WORKS! Now we can trade it with confidence knowing the statistics! 📊

📝 Part 2: Manual Backtesting Process

Step-by-Step Manual Backtesting

Step 1: Define Your Strategy Rules (Must be 100% Objective)

Example - 20/50 SMA Crossover:

  • Entry: Buy when 20 SMA crosses above 50 SMA, close above cross
  • Exit: Sell when 20 SMA crosses below 50 SMA, close below cross
  • Stop Loss: 5% below entry OR below 50 SMA (whichever is closer)
  • Position Size: Risk 2% per trade
  • Filter: Only trade when price above 200 SMA

Key: Rules must be clear enough that anyone could execute them identically!

Step 2: Choose Your Test Period & Assets

Time Period:

  • Minimum 100 trades OR 2 years of data
  • Include both bull and bear markets
  • Test recent data (last 3-5 years most relevant)

Asset Selection:

  • Test on 3-5 different stocks/assets
  • Include different sectors (tech, finance, energy)
  • Test on both trending and ranging stocks

Step 3: Open Historical Charts & Apply Strategy

Process:

  1. Go to your trading platform
  2. Open daily chart and scroll back to start date
  3. Add your indicators (20 SMA, 50 SMA, 200 SMA)
  4. Scroll forward ONE candle at a time
  5. When signal appears, record the trade
  6. Continue until you reach present day

Pro Tip: Use "bar replay" feature if your platform has it!

Step 4: Record Every Trade

Document for each trade:

  • Trade #
  • Date of entry
  • Entry price
  • Stop loss price
  • Exit price
  • Date of exit
  • Profit/Loss in $ and %
  • Win or Loss
  • Notes (what happened, why it won/lost)

Tool: Use Excel/Google Sheets or a trading journal app

Step 5: Calculate Performance Metrics

After recording all trades, calculate:

  • Win Rate = Wins ÷ Total Trades × 100
  • Average Win = Total Win $ ÷ Number of Wins
  • Average Loss = Total Loss $ ÷ Number of Losses
  • Profit Factor = Total Wins $ ÷ Total Loss $ (must be >1.5)
  • Max Drawdown = Largest peak-to-trough decline
  • Risk-Reward Ratio = Avg Win ÷ Avg Loss

📊 Complete Backtest Example

Strategy: Bull Flag Pattern + 61.8% Fib Retracement

Test: Apple (AAPL) 2022-2024


Results:

  • Total Trades: 23
  • Winners: 15 (65% win rate) ✅
  • Losers: 8 (35% loss rate)
  • Average Win: +6.2%
  • Average Loss: -2.8%
  • R:R Ratio: 2.2:1 ✅
  • Profit Factor: 2.8 (excellent!) ✅
  • Max Consecutive Losses: 3
  • Max Drawdown: -11%
  • Net Return: +68% in 2 years! 🚀

Verdict: Strategy VALIDATED! Ready to trade live with confidence!

📊 Part 3: Performance Metrics Explained

Key Metrics Every Trader Must Know

1️⃣ Win Rate (Hit Rate)

Formula: (Number of Wins ÷ Total Trades) × 100

Example: 60 wins out of 100 trades = 60% win rate


Good vs Bad:

  • Below 40% = Poor strategy ❌
  • 40-50% = Acceptable if great R:R ratio
  • 50-60% = Good ✅
  • 60-70% = Excellent ✅✅
  • Above 70% = Exceptional (or curve-fitted) 🌟

Remember: Even 40% win rate is profitable if R:R is 1:3+!

2️⃣ Profit Factor

Formula: Total Profit $ ÷ Total Loss $

Example: Made $10,000, Lost $4,000 = 2.5 profit factor


Interpretation:

  • Below 1.0 = Losing system ❌
  • 1.0-1.5 = Barely profitable
  • 1.5-2.0 = Good system ✅
  • 2.0-3.0 = Excellent system ✅✅
  • Above 3.0 = Outstanding 🌟

Meaning: 2.5 profit factor = For every $1 lost, you make $2.50!

3️⃣ Maximum Drawdown

Definition: Largest peak-to-trough decline in account value

Example: Account hit $12,000 high, then dropped to $9,600 = 20% drawdown


Importance:

  • Shows worst-case scenario you must survive
  • Tests your psychological resilience
  • If backtest shows 30% drawdown, can you handle that live?

Guidelines:

  • Below 15% = Excellent ✅
  • 15-25% = Acceptable
  • Above 25% = High risk ⚠️
  • Above 40% = Dangerous system ❌

4️⃣ Average Risk-Reward Ratio

Formula: Average Win $ ÷ Average Loss $

Example: Avg win $300, Avg loss $150 = 2:1 R:R


Why It Matters:

  • You can be wrong 60% and still profit with 1:3 R:R!
  • Low win rate + high R:R = Still profitable
  • High win rate + low R:R = Can still lose money

Minimum Standards:

  • 1:1 R:R = Need 55%+ win rate ❌
  • 1:2 R:R = Need 40%+ win rate ✅
  • 1:3 R:R = Need 30%+ win rate ✅✅

🎯 The Math of Profitability

Scenario A: High Win Rate, Low R:R

  • Win Rate: 70%
  • R:R: 1:0.8 (avg win $80, avg loss $100)
  • 100 trades: 70 wins × $80 = $5,600 profit
  • 30 losses × $100 = $3,000 loss
  • Net: +$2,600 (26% return) ✅

Scenario B: Low Win Rate, High R:R

  • Win Rate: 40%
  • R:R: 1:3 (avg win $300, avg loss $100)
  • 100 trades: 40 wins × $300 = $12,000 profit
  • 60 losses × $100 = $6,000 loss
  • Net: +$6,000 (60% return) ✅✅

Lesson: Scenario B with LOWER win rate makes MORE money due to superior R:R! 🎯

🔄 Part 4: Chart Review & Strategy Improvement

Learning from Your Backtest

Review Process - After Backtesting

  1. Identify best trades: What made them work?
  2. Identify worst trades: What patterns caused losses?
  3. Look for edge: Does strategy work better in certain conditions?
  4. Optimize rules: Can filters improve win rate without reducing trades?
  5. Test improvements: Re-backtest with new rules

🚨 Common Backtesting Mistakes

  • Curve Fitting (Over-Optimization): Creating strategy that works ONLY on past data, won't work forward
  • Look-Ahead Bias: Using information not available at time of trade (future data)
  • Survivorship Bias: Only testing stocks that still exist (ignoring bankruptcies)
  • Cherry Picking: Only counting trades that support your theory
  • Too Few Trades: Testing on only 20 trades (need 100+ for statistical relevance)
  • Ignoring Costs: Not accounting for commissions, slippage, spreads

✅ Signs of a Robust Strategy

  • Works across multiple assets: Not just 1 stock
  • Works in different market conditions: Bull, bear, sideways
  • Works on different timeframes: Daily, 4H, 1H
  • 100+ trades tested: Statistical significance
  • Profit Factor >1.5: Consistent edge
  • Max Drawdown <20%: Survivable losses
  • Simple rules: Not 15 conditions (complexity = curve fitting)

💡 Strategy Optimization Example

Original Strategy: 20/50 SMA crossover (no filter)

  • 100 trades, 52% win rate, 1.4 profit factor ❌ (barely profitable)

Observation: Many false signals in ranging markets


Improved Strategy: Added 200 SMA trend filter (only trade with trend)

  • 68 trades (fewer but better quality)
  • 64% win rate ✅ (up from 52%)
  • 2.1 profit factor ✅✅ (up from 1.4)
  • Max drawdown -12% (down from -22%)

Result: One simple filter improved EVERYTHING! This is how pros optimize! 🎯

📚 Extra Learning Notes

📖 Backtesting Best Practices

  • Always test on out-of-sample data (data not used in optimization)
  • Walk-forward testing: test on Period 1, optimize, test on Period 2
  • Account for realistic slippage (1-3 ticks)
  • Include commission costs
  • Test during high volatility periods

⏰ Sample Sizes Matter

  • 20-50 trades = Insufficient ❌
  • 50-100 trades = Minimum ✅
  • 100-200 trades = Good ✅✅
  • 200+ trades = Excellent 🌟
  • More trades = More confidence

💡 Pro Tip: Forward Testing

After backtesting, always:

  • Paper trade for 30 days
  • Track performance
  • If matches backtest = Go live
  • If different = Re-analyze strategy

🎯 Monte Carlo Analysis

Advanced technique:

  • Randomize trade order
  • Run 1000+ simulations
  • Calculate probability of drawdown
  • See worst-case scenarios

📈 Expectancy Formula

E = (Win% × Avg Win) - (Loss% × Avg Loss)

Positive expectancy = Edge

Example: (0.6 × $300) - (0.4 × $150) = $120 per trade expected!

⚠️ Market Regime Changes

  • Strategies work in certain markets
  • Trend strategies fail in ranges
  • Mean reversion fails in trends
  • Always check current regime
  • Adapt or sit out

🎓 Advanced: Creating a Backtesting Checklist

Before declaring strategy valid, check:

  1. ✅ 100+ trades tested
  2. ✅ Profit factor >1.5
  3. ✅ Win rate >40% (or great R:R)
  4. ✅ Max drawdown <20%
  5. ✅ Works on 3+ assets
  6. ✅ Works in bull AND bear markets
  7. ✅ Rules are 100% objective
  8. ✅ Positive expectancy
  9. ✅ Realistic costs included
  10. ✅ No curve fitting (tested out-of-sample)

If all check = Strategy is VALIDATED! 🎯
