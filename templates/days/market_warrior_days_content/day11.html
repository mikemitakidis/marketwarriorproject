📊 Part 1: RSI (Relative Strength Index)

What is RSI?

RSI is a momentum oscillator that measures the speed and magnitude of price movements. It ranges from 0-100 and tells you if a stock is overbought (too expensive) or oversold (too cheap).

🎯 How RSI Works (Simple Explanation)

Imagine a rubber band: When you stretch it too far up (RSI > 70), it wants to snap back down. When you stretch it too far down (RSI < 30), it wants to snap back up. RSI measures how "stretched" price momentum is!


Calculation (You don't need to memorize this):

RSI = 100 - (100 / (1 + RS))

Where RS = Average Gain / Average Loss over 14 periods

Your chart software calculates this automatically!

The Three RSI Zones

🔴 Overbought Zone (70-100)

Meaning: Price has risen too fast, too far

Psychology: Bulls exhausted, sellers preparing

Signal: Potential reversal DOWN or pullback coming

Action: Consider selling, tighten stops, take profits

Warning: In strong uptrends, can stay >70 for weeks!

⚪ Neutral Zone (30-70)

Meaning: Normal, healthy price action

Psychology: Balanced buying/selling pressure

Signal: No extreme condition, wait for setup

Action: Use other indicators, watch for zone exits

🟢 Oversold Zone (0-30)

Meaning: Price has fallen too fast, too far

Psychology: Bears exhausted, buyers preparing

Signal: Potential reversal UP or bounce coming

Action: Look for buying opportunities

Warning: In strong downtrends, can stay <30 for weeks!

📈 Part 2: MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)

What is MACD?

MACD measures momentum by comparing two moving averages (fast vs slow). It shows when momentum is building (convergence) or fading (divergence), helping you catch trends early!

🎯 MACD Components (3 Parts)

1. MACD Line (Blue): Difference between 12 EMA and 26 EMA

2. Signal Line (Red/Orange): 9 EMA of the MACD line

3. Histogram (Bars): Distance between MACD and Signal line


Simple Translation: When blue crosses above red = Bullish momentum building. When blue crosses below red = Bearish momentum building. Histogram shows strength!

Reading MACD Signals

🟢 Bullish MACD Cross

Signal: MACD line crosses ABOVE signal line

Meaning: Bullish momentum accelerating

Best When: Happens below zero line (oversold bounce)

Confirmation: Histogram turning green/positive

Action: Buy signal (wait for MA confirmation)

🔴 Bearish MACD Cross

Signal: MACD line crosses BELOW signal line

Meaning: Bearish momentum accelerating

Best When: Happens above zero line (overbought rejection)

Confirmation: Histogram turning red/negative

Action: Sell signal (wait for MA confirmation)

⚡ Histogram Expansion

Signal: Histogram bars getting larger

Meaning: Momentum strengthening (trend accelerating)

Green bars growing: Strong uptrend

Red bars growing: Strong downtrend

Action: Add to position, let winners run

⚠️ Histogram Contraction

Signal: Histogram bars getting smaller

Meaning: Momentum fading (trend weakening)

Warning Sign: Potential reversal approaching

Action: Tighten stops, consider taking profits

🔄 Part 3: Using MAs to Confirm RSI & MACD

The Triple Confirmation System

This is where everything clicks! Individual indicators give signals, but Moving Averages confirm if those signals align with the bigger trend. Never trade RSI or MACD alone - always check MAs first!

🎯 The Pro Trading Decision Tree

  1. Step 1: Check MA Trend
    • Price above 200 SMA = Uptrend (look for buys)
    • Price below 200 SMA = Downtrend (look for sells)

  2. Step 2: RSI Confirmation
    • Uptrend: Wait for RSI <40 (oversold in uptrend)
    • Downtrend: Wait for RSI >60 (overbought in downtrend)

  3. Step 3: MACD Confirmation
    • Uptrend: Buy when MACD crosses up
    • Downtrend: Sell when MACD crosses down

  4. Step 4: Entry Near MA Support/Resistance
    • Uptrend: Enter near 20 or 50 SMA
    • Downtrend: Enter near 20 or 50 SMA rejection

All 4 align = 80%+ probability trade!

✅ Perfect Buy Setup

Checklist:

  • ✅ Price above 200 SMA (uptrend)
  • ✅ RSI drops to 35 (oversold)
  • ✅ MACD bullish cross below zero
  • ✅ Price at 50 SMA support

Result: 4/4 confirmations = ENTER!

⚠️ Mixed Signals (WAIT)

Scenario:

  • ✅ Price above 200 SMA (uptrend)
  • ✅ RSI at 28 (oversold)
  • ❌ MACD bearish cross (conflicting!)
  • ✅ Price at 50 SMA

Result: 3/4 confirmations = SKIP!

❌ Low Probability (AVOID)

Scenario:

  • ❌ Price below 200 SMA (downtrend)
  • ✅ RSI at 25 (oversold)
  • ❌ MACD still bearish
  • ❌ No nearby MA support

Result: 1/4 confirmations = DON'T TRADE!

⚠️ Common Indicator Mistakes

🌍 Part 4: Economic Cycle & Indicator Behavior

Understanding the Economic Cycle

The economy moves in predictable cycles, and indicators behave differently in each phase. Knowing which phase we're in dramatically improves your indicator interpretation!

📊 The 4 Economic Phases

📈 Expansion (Bull Market)

Economy: Growing, jobs increasing

Stocks: Rising steadily

Indicator Behavior:

  • RSI often >50, stays elevated
  • MACD positive, expanding
  • MAs rising, price above all MAs

Strategy: Buy dips (RSI <40), ride uptrend

🔥 Peak (Late Bull)

Economy: Overheating, inflation rising

Stocks: Topping, volatility increasing

Indicator Behavior:

  • RSI >70 frequently (overbought)
  • MACD divergences appearing
  • Price far above MAs (stretched)

Strategy: Take profits, tighten stops

📉 Contraction (Bear Market)

Economy: Shrinking, job losses

Stocks: Falling steadily

Indicator Behavior:

  • RSI often <50, stays low
  • MACD negative, expanding down
  • MAs falling, price below all MAs

Strategy: Short rallies (RSI >60), cash is king

💙 Trough (Bottom)

Economy: Stabilizing, worst over

Stocks: Bottoming, finding support

Indicator Behavior:

  • RSI <30 frequently (oversold)
  • MACD bullish divergences
  • Price finding support at 200 SMA

Strategy: Accumulate quality stocks, long-term buy

🎯 How to Trade Each Cycle Phase

Expansion Phase (Best for Trading):

  • RSI dips to 30-40 = Strong buy signals
  • MACD bullish crosses work reliably
  • MA pullbacks = Perfect entries
  • Strategy: Stay mostly long, buy every dip

Peak Phase (Be Defensive):

  • RSI >70 = Real warning (not "stay overbought")
  • Watch for MACD divergences carefully
  • Price far from MAs = Danger zone
  • Strategy: Take profits, reduce position sizes

Contraction Phase (Preserve Capital):

  • RSI <30 = NOT automatic buy (can go lower!)
  • MACD bearish crosses very reliable
  • MA resistance = Short opportunities
  • Strategy: Short rallies, stay mostly cash

Trough Phase (Accumulation):

  • RSI <30 + divergence = Generational buying opportunity
  • MACD bullish crosses = Start of new bull market
  • 200 SMA support = Major turning points
  • Strategy: Dollar-cost average into quality stocks
