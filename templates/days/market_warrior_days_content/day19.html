📝 Part 1: What is a Trading Plan?

Definition

A Trading Plan is a comprehensive written document that outlines your trading strategy, risk management rules, goals, and procedures. It's your business plan for trading - a systematic approach to the markets that removes emotion and guesswork.

🎯 Key Components of Every Trading Plan:

  1. Personal Information: Goals, risk tolerance, time commitment
  2. Strategy Rules: Entry, exit, and management criteria
  3. Risk Management: Position sizing, stop losses, max drawdown
  4. Markets & Instruments: What you trade and why
  5. Trading Schedule: When you trade (time of day, days of week)
  6. Performance Metrics: How you measure success
  7. Rules for Different Scenarios: Bull/bear/sideways markets
  8. Psychological Rules: How to handle wins/losses

Why You MUST Have a Trading Plan

1. Removes Emotion

When your plan says "If price breaks support with volume, sell" - you sell. No fear, no hope, no hesitation. The plan decides, not your emotions!

2. Provides Consistency

Same strategy, same rules, every single time. This allows you to measure what works and improve systematically.

3. Builds Discipline

Your plan is your commitment. Following it builds the discipline needed for long-term success.

4. Allows Backtesting & Improvement

Clear rules = you can test them. Vague "feelings" = impossible to measure or improve.

5. Protects Your Capital

Pre-defined risk limits prevent devastating losses. Your plan includes max loss per trade, per day, per week.

🚨 Trading Without a Plan = Gambling

No plan scenario: "I think this stock might go up, let me buy some... oh it's dropping, should I sell? Maybe it'll come back... okay I'll hold... PANIC SELL at the bottom!"

With plan scenario: "My plan says: Buy when RSI <30 AND price above 50 MA. Entry $50, stop loss $48 (4% risk), target $56 (12% gain). Risk 1% of account = 25 shares. Execute." The difference is MASSIVE!

🎯 Part 2: Building Your Trading Plan

Section 1: Personal Profile & Goals

📊 Trading Goals (Be Specific!):
❌ Bad: "Make money"
✅ Good: "Generate 15% annual return with max 10% drawdown"
💰 Starting Capital:
How much money are you starting with? (Never risk money you can't afford to lose)
⏰ Time Commitment:
How many hours per day/week can you dedicate to trading?
(This determines if you're a day trader, swing trader, or position trader)
🎚️ Risk Tolerance:
Conservative (0.5-1% risk per trade) | Moderate (1-2%) | Aggressive (2-3%)
🎓 Experience Level:
Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced (determines complexity of strategies you use)

Section 2: Strategy Rules (The Most Important Part!)

📋 Your strategy rules must be 100% OBJECTIVE

No room for interpretation! Anyone should be able to read your rules and take the exact same trades.

Entry Rules (When to BUY/SELL)

Example Entry Rules:

  • ✅ "Buy when: Price above 50-day MA + RSI below 40 + MACD bullish crossover"
  • ✅ "Sell when: Price breaks below support + RSI above 60 + volume >1.5x avg"
  • ❌ "Buy when it feels right" (TOO VAGUE!)
  • ❌ "Sell when I think it'll drop" (NOT OBJECTIVE!)

Exit Rules (When to Close the Trade)

3 Types of Exits:

  1. Stop Loss (Loss Exit): "Exit if price hits 3% below entry" or "Exit if price closes below 50 MA"
  2. Take Profit (Win Exit): "Exit at 2:1 R:R" or "Exit at resistance level"
  3. Time Exit: "Exit if no movement within 5 days" or "Exit all Friday before close"

Position Sizing Rules

Define exactly how many shares/units to buy:

Example: "Risk 1% of capital per trade. Position size = (Account × 1%) ÷ (Entry - Stop Loss)"

If account = $10,000, entry = $50, stop = $48:
Position size = ($10,000 × 1%) ÷ ($50 - $48) = $100 ÷ $2 = 50 shares

Section 3: Risk Management Rules

🛡️ Your Risk Limits (NEVER BREAK THESE!)

Max Risk Per Trade: 1-2% of capital (1% for beginners)
Max Daily Loss: 3% of capital (stop trading for the day)
Max Weekly Loss: 6% of capital (stop trading for the week)
Max Drawdown: 15-20% (stop trading, re-evaluate strategy)
Max Open Positions: 3-5 trades at once (prevents over-exposure)
Max Correlated Trades: Don't buy 5 tech stocks at once (diversify!)

Section 4: Markets & Instruments

📈 What You Trade

Define clearly:

  • Asset Classes: Stocks? Forex? Crypto? Commodities?
  • Specific Markets: S&P 500 stocks? EUR/USD? Bitcoin?
  • Timeframes: 5-min charts? Daily charts? 4-hour?
  • Market Conditions: Trending markets only? Any condition?
  • Liquidity Requirements: Only trade assets with >$1M daily volume

Section 5: Trading Schedule

⏰ When You Trade

Be specific about timing:

  • Days: "Monday-Friday only" or "Tuesday-Thursday only"
  • Hours: "9:30am-11:30am EST (market open)" or "2pm-4pm (market close)"
  • Session: "US session for forex" or "After earnings for stocks"
  • Frequency: "Check charts 3x per day" or "Place orders Sunday night"
  • Avoid: "No trading on major news days" or "No Friday afternoon trades"

Section 6: Performance Metrics & Goals

📊 How You Measure Success

Track these metrics weekly/monthly:

  • Win Rate: Target >50% (or >40% with great R:R)
  • Profit Factor: Target >1.5 (gross profit ÷ gross loss)
  • Average Win:Loss Ratio: Target >1.5:1
  • Max Drawdown: Stay under 15-20%
  • Monthly ROI: Target 3-8% per month (realistic!)
  • Sharpe Ratio: Target >1.0 (risk-adjusted returns)

Section 7: Psychological Rules

🧠 Emotional Management Rules

After a winning trade:

  • ✅ Follow your plan (don't overtrade from confidence)
  • ✅ Take partial profits if plan allows
  • ✅ Review what made the trade successful
  • ❌ Don't increase position size (revenge greed)

After a losing trade:

  • ✅ Review if you followed your rules
  • ✅ If rules followed = accept loss (part of business)
  • ✅ Take a 15-min break before next trade
  • ❌ Don't revenge trade (doubling position to recover)
  • ❌ Don't abandon your plan

Circuit Breakers:

  • After 3 losses in a row → Stop trading for the day
  • If feeling emotional (angry, euphoric, fearful) → Step away
  • If max daily loss hit → STOP (no exceptions!)

💰 Part 3: Understanding ROI (Return on Investment)

What is ROI?

ROI (Return on Investment) measures the profitability of your trading. It's calculated as:

📐 ROI Formula

ROI = [(Final Value - Initial Investment) ÷ Initial Investment] × 100%

Example:

Start with $10,000, end with $11,500:

ROI = [($11,500 - $10,000) ÷ $10,000] × 100% = 15% ROI

Realistic ROI Expectations

📊 What's Realistic?

  • Beginner Traders: 5-15% annual ROI (if profitable at all)
  • Intermediate Traders: 15-30% annual ROI
  • Professional Traders: 20-50% annual ROI
  • Elite Traders: 50%+ annual ROI (very rare!)

Context: Warren Buffett averages ~20% annually over 50+ years. The S&P 500 averages ~10% annually. If you beat the market, you're doing well!

⚠️ Beware of Unrealistic Promises!

Anyone promising "200% monthly returns" or "10x your money in 3 months" is either:

  • 1. Scamming you
  • 2. Taking insane risks that will blow up eventually
  • 3. Trading with extremely high leverage (gambling)

Sustainable trading = realistic returns with managed risk!

Setting Your ROI Goals

🎯 SMART Goals for Trading ROI

  • Specific: "Achieve 20% annual ROI"
  • Measurable: "Track monthly returns, target 1.5% per month"
  • Achievable: "Based on 55% win rate + 1.5:1 R:R = realistic"
  • Relevant: "Aligns with 3-year goal to grow $10k to $17k"
  • Time-bound: "Review quarterly, adjust strategy if off track"
