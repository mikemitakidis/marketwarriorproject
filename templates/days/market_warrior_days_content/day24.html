💡 Why Rebalancing Matters

A real example:

  • January: Your portfolio is 60% stocks, 40% bonds
  • December: After stock gains, it's now 75% stocks, 25% bonds
  • Your risk increased without you realizing!
  • Rebalancing = selling winners, buying losers = disciplined profit-taking

Studies show: Rebalanced portfolios earn 1.5% more annually with less risk!

🔄 Part 2: When & How to Rebalance

Rebalancing Triggers

📅 Calendar Method

Rebalance every:

  • • Quarterly (4x/year)
  • • Semi-annually (2x/year)
  • • Annually (1x/year)

Most popular: Annual

📊 Threshold Method

Rebalance when allocation drifts:

  • • 5% from target (aggressive)
  • • 10% from target (moderate)
  • • 15% from target (relaxed)

Recommended: 10%

🎯 Hybrid Method

Check quarterly, act if:

  • • 12 months passed OR
  • • 10% drift occurred
  • • Major market event

Best of both!

The Rebalancing Process

✅ Step-by-Step Rebalancing

  1. Calculate current allocation: What % is each asset now?
  2. Compare to target: How far off are you?
  3. Identify trades needed: What to sell/buy?
  4. Execute systematically: Sell winners first, then buy losers
  5. Document everything: Track for tax and performance

💡 Pro Tip: Use New Money to Rebalance

Instead of selling (triggers taxes/fees), use new deposits to buy underweight assets. This is called "rebalancing through contributions"!

💰 Part 3: Tax-Efficient Rebalancing

⚠️ Tax Considerations (For Future Reference)

When you move from demo to real trading, remember:

  • Selling winners = capital gains tax
  • Use tax-advantaged accounts when possible
  • Consider tax-loss harvesting
  • Rebalance less frequently in taxable accounts

For now: Practice freely on demo without tax concerns!

Smart Rebalancing Strategies

🎯 Strategy 1: Core-Satellite Approach

  • Core (80%): Index funds, rarely rebalanced
  • Satellite (20%): Active trades, frequently adjusted

Benefit: Reduces taxes while staying flexible

🎯 Strategy 2: Tolerance Bands

  • Set wider bands for volatile assets (±15%)
  • Tighter bands for stable assets (±5%)
  • Only rebalance assets outside bands

Benefit: Reduces unnecessary trading

🎯 Strategy 3: Opportunistic Rebalancing

  • Rebalance during market extremes
  • Buy during crashes (March 2020)
  • Sell during euphoria (2021 peaks)

Benefit: Enhanced returns through timing
