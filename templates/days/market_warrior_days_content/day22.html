🎪 Part 1: IPOs (Initial Public Offerings)

What is an IPO?

IPO Trading Strategies

✅ The "Wait and See" Strategy (Safest)

  • NEVER buy on IPO day (too volatile)
  • Wait 30-90 days for price to stabilize
  • Look for base formation around support
  • Enter after first earnings report

Example: Facebook IPO at $38, dropped to $18, now worth $500+

❌ IPO Day Trading (Extremely Risky)

  • Buy at open if opens below IPO price
  • Sell into first spike (usually 10-30 minutes)
  • Never hold overnight on day 1
  • Use only 1-2% of portfolio maximum

Where to Trade IPOs

Best platforms for IPO access:

  • eToro: Often lists major IPOs on day one, easy access for retail traders
  • Interactive Brokers: Sometimes offers pre-IPO access
  • Robinhood: IPO access program for active users
  • TD Ameritrade: IPO center with allocation possibility

💰 Part 2: Penny Stocks (Under $5)

🚨 EXTREME WARNING

95% of penny stocks are scams or failing companies. Most go to $0. Only trade with money you're prepared to lose entirely!

Why Penny Stocks Move

  • Low float: Few shares = easy to manipulate
  • News catalysts: Any news causes 50-200% moves
  • Pump & dump schemes: Coordinated buying then selling
  • Short squeezes: Like GameStop phenomenon

Penny Stock Patterns

Pattern What Happens Success Rate Risk Level
Morning Spike Gaps up 30-100% at open 60% fade by noon Extreme
Breakout Breaks 52-week high 30% continue up Very High
Bounce Play Bounces off support 40% success High
News Catalyst FDA approval, contract 50% hold gains Very High

Penny Stock Rules

🛡️ Survival Rules

  1. NEVER hold overnight (90% gap down)
  2. Use stop loss at -10% maximum
  3. Take profits at +20-30%
  4. Trade only with high volume (1M+ shares)
  5. Avoid anything under $0.50
  6. Maximum 2% of portfolio per trade

₿ Part 3: Cryptocurrency Trading

Understanding Crypto Volatility

Why crypto is so volatile:

  • 24/7 trading (never closes)
  • No circuit breakers (can drop 90% in hours)
  • Highly speculative (no real valuations)
  • Whale manipulation (big holders control price)
  • News-driven (Elon tweet = 30% move)

Major Cryptocurrencies

Crypto Volatility Use Case Risk Level
Bitcoin (BTC) 20-30% weekly Digital gold High
Ethereum (ETH) 30-40% weekly Smart contracts High
Altcoins 50-100% daily Various Extreme
Meme Coins 100-500% daily None/Speculation Gambling

Crypto Trading Strategies

📊 Strategy 1: Trend Following

  • Buy when breaks above 20-day MA
  • Hold while trend intact
  • Sell when breaks below 20-day MA
  • Works best with Bitcoin/Ethereum

🎯 Strategy 2: Range Trading

  • Identify support/resistance levels
  • Buy at support, sell at resistance
  • Use 5-10% profit targets
  • Stop loss 3% below support

Where to Trade Crypto

Recommended platforms:

  • eToro: User-friendly, copy crypto traders, major coins only (safer)
  • Coinbase: Beginner-friendly, insured, higher fees
  • Binance: Most coins, lowest fees, complex interface
  • Kraken: Good security, margin trading available

⚡ Part 4: Scalping & Volatility Trading

🎯 Scalping Strategy (1-5 Minute Trades)

What is scalping? Making 10-50 trades per day, holding for seconds to minutes, targeting 0.5-2% gains per trade.

The 3-Candle Scalping System

  1. Setup: 1-minute chart, 20 EMA, RSI, Volume
  2. Entry: When 3 green candles close above 20 EMA + RSI > 50
  3. Target: 0.5-1% gain (5-10 cents on $10 stock)
  4. Stop: 0.25% loss (immediate exit)
  5. Time limit: Exit after 5 minutes regardless

Success rate: 60-70% wins, but need high win rate due to commissions

Requirements for Demo Practice:

  • Fast execution platform (eToro, Trading 212, etc.)
  • Level 2 data if available (order book)
  • No emotion (mechanical execution)
  • Practice on demo for minimum 3 months first!

Volatility Indicators

VIX - The Fear Index

VIX <20 (Low Vol) VIX 20-30 (Normal) VIX >30 (High Vol)

Trading rules:

  • VIX <15: Reduced position sizes (calm market)
  • VIX 15-25: Normal trading
  • VIX 25-35: Increased opportunities (bigger moves)
  • VIX >35: Extreme caution (market panic)

Best Volatile Stocks for Scalping

  • Tesla (TSLA): Moves $5-20 daily
  • NVIDIA (NVDA): Tech volatility champion
  • AMC/GME: Meme stock volatility
  • SPDR S&P 500 (SPY): Most liquid, tight spreads
  • Crypto stocks (MARA, RIOT): Follow Bitcoin

📊 Part 5: Managing Volatility Risk

☠️ The Volatility Death Spiral

  1. Big gain → Overconfidence → Bigger position
  2. Big loss → Revenge trade → Even bigger position
  3. Bigger loss → Panic → All-in desperation trade
  4. Account blown → Game over

Solution: Fixed position sizes regardless of wins/losses!
