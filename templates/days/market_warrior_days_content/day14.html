📋 Part 1: Pre-Trade Checklist System

The Professional Trader's Checklist

Before entering ANY trade, check ALL boxes below. If even ONE box is unchecked - DON'T TRADE!

1. Trend Confirmation (Day 8 + 11)

Is price above/below key moving averages? What's the trend direction?

2. Support/Resistance Level (Day 9)

Am I buying at support in uptrend or selling at resistance in downtrend?

3. Pattern Recognition (Day 10)

Is there a valid chart pattern? (Flag, triangle, H&S, double top/bottom)

4. Indicator Confirmation (Day 11)

RSI in good zone? MACD showing momentum? Price near key MA?

5. Fundamental Check (Day 12-13)

For stocks: Is company fundamentally sound? Not overvalued?

6. Risk Management Plan (Day 13)

Entry, stop loss, target defined? Risk 1-2%? Risk/Reward minimum 1:2?

Step 3: Plan Risk & Position Size (5 minutes)

  • Make sure you're risking only 1-2% of account

Step 4: Execute Trade (2 minutes)

🎯 Part 4: What Makes A Good Trade?

✅ Signs of a HIGH Quality Trade:

  • 4+ Confirmations: Multiple signals align (trend + pattern + indicator + S/R)
  • Clear Entry Trigger: You know EXACTLY when to enter (breakout, bounce, cross)
  • Obvious Stop Loss: Natural place to exit if wrong (below support, above resistance)
  • Good R:R Ratio: Minimum 1:2, ideally 1:3 or better
  • Emotional Clarity: You feel calm and confident, not desperate or FOMO
  • Plan B Ready: You know what to do if trade goes wrong

❌ Signs of a POOR Quality Trade:

  • Few Confirmations: Only 1-2 signals (trend only, or just a pattern)
  • Vague Entry: "I'll buy somewhere around here" = Recipe for disaster
  • No Clear Stop: "I'll just hold if it drops" = How accounts die
  • Bad R:R: Risk $100 to make $50 = Losing math
  • Emotional Trade: FOMO, revenge trading, or "trying to make back losses"
  • No Plan: Don't know what you'll do if it moves against you

If you see these signs = DON'T TRADE! Wait for a better setup.

💡 Quality vs Quantity

Amateur Trader: Takes 20 trades per day, wins 30%, loses money overall

Professional Trader: Takes 2-3 trades per week, wins 60%, makes consistent money


The Lesson: You don't get paid for the number of trades. You get paid for taking ONLY the highest quality setups. One perfect trade beats 10 mediocre ones!
