🚫 Part 2: Eliminating What Fails

❌ The Hardest Part: Letting Go

If your data shows consistent losses from certain strategies, STOP using them - even if they "should" work!

Common Losing Patterns to Cut

📉 Overtrading

Sign: Win rate drops after 3+ trades

Fix: Hard limit of 2-3 trades/day

😤 Revenge Trading

Sign: Bigger losses follow initial loss

Fix: Stop after 2 consecutive losses

⏰ Bad Timing

Sign: Specific hours show losses

Fix: Trade only profitable hours

📊 Wrong Markets

Sign: Certain assets always lose

Fix: Stick to winning assets

🎲 No Edge Trades

Sign: Random entry trades fail

Fix: Strict entry criteria

📱 FOMO Entries

Sign: Chase trades lose

Fix: Wait for pullbacks

🎯 Part 3: The Optimization Process

Strategy Adjustment Decision Tree

Strategy has 60%+ win rate?

YES → Keep and increase allocation

NO → Continue to next check

Strategy has positive expectancy?

YES → Tweak for improvement

NO → Continue to next check

Can identify why it fails?

YES → Fix the specific issue

NO → Continue to next check

Tested for 50+ trades?

YES → Eliminate completely

NO → Test 50 more trades

Making Adjustments

✅ The A/B Testing Method

  1. Identify one variable to change (entry, exit, position size)
  2. Test original vs modified for 20 trades each
  3. Compare results objectively
  4. Keep winner, discard loser
  5. Repeat with next variable

Never change multiple variables at once!

📋 Your Optimized Trading Rules

Based on your data, create specific rules:

  • Position Sizing: Based on win rate data
  • Daily Limits: Max trades and max loss
  • Entry Criteria: Minimum confirmations required
  • Exit Rules: Based on what maximizes profits

Example Optimized System

🏆 "The Morning Momentum System"

Based on 3 months of demo data showing:

  • 65% win rate from 9:30-11:00am
  • Best with tech stocks and EUR/USD
  • Breakout patterns most profitable

Optimized Rules:

  1. Trade only 9:30-11:00am EST
  2. Focus: AAPL, TSLA, NVDA, EUR/USD
  3. Setup: Breakout above previous day high
  4. Confirmation: Volume spike + RSI >60
  5. Risk: 0.5% per trade (smaller size, higher win rate)
  6. Target: 1.5R minimum (based on average winner)
  7. Max 2 trades per day
  8. Stop if down 1% for the day

🔄 The Monthly Evolution Process

  1. Week 1-3: Execute current system strictly

  3. Identify: One thing to improve

  5. Compare: Results vs previous month
  6. Decide: Keep or revert change
