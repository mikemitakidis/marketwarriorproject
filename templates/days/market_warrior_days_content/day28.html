🎯 Part 1: Start Small - The Professional's Path

💰 Why Starting Small Wins

Professional trader progression:

  • Months 1-6: Demo only ($100k virtual)
  • Months 7-12: $500-$1000 real account
  • Year 2: $5,000-$10,000 (if profitable)
  • Year 3+: Scale gradually with profits

Remember: 90% who start big lose everything in 90 days!

⚠️ The Small Account Advantage

  • Less emotional pressure = Better decisions
  • Mistakes are cheap lessons
  • Focus on % returns, not $ amounts
  • Build confidence without fear
  • Develop discipline when stakes are low

✅ Starting Small Success Formula

  1. Master one strategy completely
  2. Trade one market initially
  3. Risk only 1% per trade
  4. Focus on consistency, not home runs
  5. Reinvest profits, withdraw none for 1 year

📊 Part 3: Global Economic Indicators

1️⃣ GDP (Gross Domestic Product)

What it measures: Total economic output and growth

Released: Quarterly (3 readings: Advance, Preliminary, Final)

Healthy range: 2-3% annual growth

Market Impact:

  • GDP ↑ Better than expected → Stocks ↑, Currency ↑
  • GDP ↓ Worse than expected → Stocks ↓, Currency ↓
  • Recession = 2 quarters negative GDP

Trading tip: Trade the revision more than initial release!

2️⃣ CPI (Consumer Price Index) - Inflation

What it measures: Price changes, inflation rate

Released: Monthly (usually 2nd week)

Target: Central banks target 2% annual

Market Impact:

  • CPI ↑ High inflation → Rates likely ↑ → Stocks ↓, Bonds ↓
  • CPI ↓ Low inflation → Rates likely ↓ → Stocks ↑, Bonds ↑
  • Gold typically rises with inflation

Trading tip: Core CPI (ex-food/energy) matters more!

3️⃣ NFP (Non-Farm Payrolls) - Jobs Report

What it measures: Monthly job creation

Released: First Friday of month, 8:30 AM EST

Healthy range: 150K-250K jobs/month

Market Impact:

  • NFP ↑ Strong jobs → USD ↑, Stocks mixed
  • NFP ↓ Weak jobs → USD ↓, Stocks ↓ (recession fear)
  • Unemployment rate equally important

Trading tip: Most volatile day of month - reduce positions!

💰 Part 2: Central Bank Indicators

Interest Rate Decisions

🏛️ Major Central Banks:

  • Federal Reserve (Fed) - USA: 8 meetings/year
  • European Central Bank (ECB) - Europe: 8 meetings/year
  • Bank of England (BoE) - UK: 8 meetings/year
  • Bank of Japan (BoJ) - Japan: 8 meetings/year

Impact:

  • Rate ↑ = Currency ↑, Stocks ↓, Bonds ↓
  • Rate ↓ = Currency ↓, Stocks ↑, Bonds ↑
  • Forward guidance often more important than actual decision

⚠️ Trading Central Bank Days

Rules for safety:

  1. Close positions before announcement
  2. Wait 30 minutes after for volatility to settle
  3. Trade the trend that emerges, not the initial spike
  4. Watch for statement changes, not just rates

📈 Part 5: Trading Economic Releases

News Trading Strategies

🎯 Strategy 1: Fade the Spike

  1. Wait for initial knee-jerk reaction
  2. Enter opposite direction after 5-15 minutes
  3. Target: 50% retracement of spike
  4. Stop: Beyond spike high/low

Best for: NFP, CPI overreactions

🎯 Strategy 2: Breakout Continuation

  1. Mark range before news
  2. Wait for clear break with volume
  3. Enter on first pullback
  4. Target: Range width projection

Best for: Central bank surprises

🎯 Strategy 3: Pre-Position (Advanced)

  1. Analyze consensus vs whisper numbers
  2. Position small 1 hour before
  3. Use options for limited risk
  4. Exit immediately on release

Warning: High risk, only with experience!
