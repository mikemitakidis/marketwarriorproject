🎯 Part 2: Understanding SMART Goals

What Makes Goals SMART?

  • S - Specific: Clear and well-defined
  • M - Measurable: Quantifiable progress
  • A - Achievable: Realistic and attainable
  • R - Relevant: Aligned with your vision
  • T - Time-bound: Clear deadline

❌ Bad Goal vs ✅ SMART Goal

❌ Bad: "I want to be a successful trader"

  • Too vague
  • Not measurable
  • No timeline

✅ SMART: "I will achieve 40% win rate with 1:2 risk-reward ratio, making 5% monthly returns on my $1000 demo account by March 31st, 2025"

  • Specific metrics
  • Measurable progress
  • Realistic targets
  • Clear deadline
