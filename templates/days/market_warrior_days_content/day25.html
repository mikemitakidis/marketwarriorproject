📝 Part 3: The Trading Journal

✅ What to Track in Your Journal

  1. Pre-Trade: Setup, reasoning, expected outcome
  2. Entry: Date, time, price, position size
  3. Management: Adjustments, emotions, observations
  4. Exit: Date, time, price, profit/loss
  5. Review: What worked, what didn't, lessons learned

Journal Entry Template

📓 Trade #001 Example

Date: 25/03/2024 | Asset: EUR/USD | Direction: Long

Setup: Bullish flag pattern at support, RSI oversold, MACD bullish cross

Entry: 1.0850 | Stop: 1.0820 | Target: 1.0920

Risk: $100 (1%) | Potential: $200 (1:2 R:R)

Result: Won +$180 (+1.8R) | Exit: 1.0910 (partial target)

Notes: Good setup, executed well. Could have held for full target.

Emotion Score: 7/10 (calm, slight FOMO on exit)

Grade: B+ (good trade, early exit reduced profit)

📈 Part 4: Performance Analysis Techniques

Weekly Performance Review

🗓️ Every Sunday Evening:

  1. Calculate metrics: Win rate, P&L, average trade
  2. Review all trades: Grade each A-F
  3. Identify patterns: What's working/failing?
  4. Emotional audit: When did emotions hurt you?
  5. Plan next week: What to improve?

Monthly Deep Dive

📊 First Day of Each Month:

  • Compare to goals: On track or behind?
  • Best/worst trades: Deep analysis
  • Strategy effectiveness: Which setups work?
  • Risk management: Following rules?
  • Market correlation: Performance vs market
  • Adjust strategy: Based on data

🎯 Part 5: Improving Based on Data

🔍 Common Patterns to Look For

  • Time of day: Do you trade better morning/afternoon?
  • Day of week: Monday vs Friday performance?
  • Asset class: Better with stocks or forex?
  • Market conditions: Trending vs ranging?
  • Position size: Do larger positions perform worse?
  • Hold time: Day trades vs swing trades?

✅ Data-Driven Improvements

Example discoveries from tracking:

  • "I lose 70% of trades on Mondays" → Don't trade Mondays
  • "My forex trades average +2%" → Focus on forex
  • "Losses are 2x when emotional" → Add emotional check
  • "Win rate drops after 3 trades" → Limit daily trades
  • "Best performance 9-11am" → Trade only those hours

Result: Performance improves 30-50% just from these adjustments!
