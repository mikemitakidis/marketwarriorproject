📱 Part 2: Copy Trading Revolution

✅ What is Copy Trading?

Definition: Automatically copying the trades of successful traders in real-time


How it works:

  1. Find successful traders on platforms
  2. Allocate demo funds to copy them

🎯 Part 3: Copy Trading Strategies

Strategy 1: Diversified Copying

The approach: Copy 3-5 traders with different styles

  • Trader 1: Conservative long-term (20% allocation)
  • Trader 2: Growth stocks specialist (20%)
  • Trader 3: Forex trader (20%)
  • Trader 4: Crypto expert (20%)
  • Keep 20% for your own trades

Benefit: Reduced risk through diversification

Strategy 2: The Learning Copy

The approach: Copy one expert in your area of interest

  • Allocate 50% to copying them
  • Watch every trade they make
  • Research why they entered/exited
  • Practice similar trades with remaining 50%

Benefit: Learn while you earn on demo!

Strategy 3: CopyPortfolios (eToro Exclusive)

The approach: Copy entire portfolios managed by eToro/partners

  • Top Trader: Copies best performers automatically
  • Market Portfolios: Themed investments (Tech, Green Energy)
  • Partner Portfolios: Created by investment firms

Benefit: Professional management without hedge fund minimums

⚠️ Part 4: Copy Trading Risks & Rules

🚨 Copy Trading Dangers

  • Past performance ≠ future results
  • Traders can have losing streaks
  • Some traders take excessive risks for visibility
  • You're responsible for all losses
  • Fees can add up (spreads, overnight fees)

✅ Copy Trading Best Practices

  1. Start on demo: Practice for 6+ months minimum
  2. Set copy stop loss: Maximum 20% loss per trader
  3. Review monthly: Stop copying underperformers
  4. Diversify: Never copy just one trader
  5. Keep learning: Don't become dependent on copying
  6. Check daily: Monitor copied positions

Review performance weekly

Stop copying if strategy changes or performance drops
