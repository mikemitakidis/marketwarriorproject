1. Programme Overview

This affiliate programme is operated using a third-party tracking provider (PromoteKit). You can earn commission on eligible referred course sales you generate using your unique link.

Commission Tiers

  • Public Affiliates (Tier B): 20% commission on eligible sales
  • Student Affiliates (Tier A): 30% commission on eligible sales (verified students only)
  • Influencer Network: Custom rates (invitation only)

2. How It Works

  • You share your unique referral link (or promo code if provided)
  • A visitor clicks and purchases the course
  • The sale is tracked and your commission is added to your balance
  • Payouts are processed on a Net-15 schedule

4. Cookie Window

Affiliate tracking uses a 30-day cookie window. If a customer clicks your link and purchases within that window on the same browser/device, the referral is usually credited to you (subject to tracking conditions above).

  • Net-15: Commissions are generated and paid on a Net-15 schedule, allowing time for refunds/chargebacks and payment confirmation

  • Currency: Payouts are processed in the currency shown in your affiliate portal (unless otherwise stated)
  • Payment Methods: PayPal mass payments and Wise batch payments supported

As per our course policy, affiliate accounts and their tracking links are active for a period of 5 months. After this time, links will expire. Affiliates are welcome to re-apply (via email) for a new term.

13. Best Practices (Recommendations)
