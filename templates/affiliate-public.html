What Is An Affiliate?

An affiliate is someone who promotes a product they believe in and earns a commission for successful tracked sales they refer.

🔗

Unique Referral Link

You receive a unique tracking link that identifies sales coming from your promotions.

🍪

30-Day Cookie Window

If a customer clicks your link and purchases within 30 days on the same browser/device, the referral is credited to you.

📅

Net-15 Payouts

Commissions are generated and paid after a verification period to allow time for refunds/chargebacks.

💳

Easy Withdrawals

Once you reach $50 minimum, request payouts via PayPal or Wise batch payments.
